Recipe for making Shukshuka

This is the shukshuka meal, it is easy to make.

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Vegetables
  • Tomatoes
  • Garnish

Instructions

  1. Satute the Veggies
  2. Add Aromatics
  3. Simmer the Sauce
  4. Crack eggs into the Sauce
  5. Cover and cook for 5-6 minute
